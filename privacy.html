Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · Applies to the ABMe iOS app and getabme.com

The short version: ABMe runs on your iPhone. Your health data and experiments stay on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you turn on sync). There are no accounts to create, no analytics, and no advertising. The only data that ever leaves your phone is an experiment you choose to publish or share.

What the app reads

ABMe asks for read access to Apple Health (HealthKit) so it can measure the outcomes of your experiments: sleep stages, heart rate, HRV, workouts, steps and other metrics you select. You choose which metrics each experiment uses, and you can change or revoke HealthKit access at any time in the iOS Settings app. ABMe does not write to Apple Health.

Where your data lives

  • On your iPhone. Experiment designs, randomization assignments, daily measurements and all statistics are stored and computed locally.
  • In your iCloud, if you enable it. ABMe can sync experiments between your own devices using Apple's CloudKit. That data sits in your private iCloud database, which Apple protects and we cannot read.
  • Nowhere else. ABMe has no servers of its own, no user accounts, no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Sharing is up to you

Two features send data off your phone, and both only when you start them:

  • Share a template or result card. A template link (abme://template/…) contains only the experiment design: its name, hypothesis, treatment and control descriptions, chosen metrics and settings. A result card is an image of your result that you share through the iOS share sheet to whichever app you pick. Neither contains your raw health data.
  • Publish an experiment. If you choose to publish, the daily values of that experiment's metrics and its treatment and control assignments become publicly visible so that others can follow it or try it themselves. Raw health data from other metrics or time periods is never included. You can stop publishing at any time.

Export and deletion

You can export all of your experiment data as CSV from the app's settings at any time. Deleting an experiment deletes its data from your device and from your iCloud sync; deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone.

This website

getabme.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may record standard server logs, such as your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. The early access form is hosted on Google Forms; what you enter there is handled under Google's privacy policy and is used only to contact you about the beta.

Children

ABMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
